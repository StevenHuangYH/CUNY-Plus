# CUNY Plus

CUNY Plus helps students identify the RateMyProfessors profile associated with an instructor on a CUNY course listing.

## Language

**Course instructor**:
The person named as an instructor on a course listing. The listing's name is evidence of identity, not proof that a particular RateMyProfessors profile belongs to that person.
_Avoid_: Matched professor (before identification)

**Course campus**:
The CUNY institution associated with the instructor's course listing. It is distinct from the school recorded on a candidate profile.
_Avoid_: Profile school (for the course campus)

**Professor candidate**:
A RateMyProfessors profile returned as a possible match for a course instructor, identified by its professor name, profile school, and profile link.
_Avoid_: Match (before identification)

**Automatic match**:
A professor candidate whose name and profile school unambiguously correspond to the course instructor and course campus.
_Avoid_: First result, best guess

**User-selected candidate**:
A professor candidate explicitly chosen by the student when the available identity evidence does not establish an automatic match.
_Avoid_: Automatic match, verified identity

**Professor rating**:
The rating information attached to a specific RateMyProfessors profile. Its attribution is to that profile's professor name and school.
_Avoid_: Course rating
